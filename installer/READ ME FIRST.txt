PLUCK: paste a video link inside Premiere Pro, get the video in your project.

Needs Premiere Pro 2022 or newer.


INSTALL

1. Unzip this download (don't run anything from inside the zip).
2. Close Premiere Pro.
3. Double-click the installer:
     Mac:      "Install Pluck.command"
     Windows:  "Install Pluck.bat"
4. Open Premiere Pro, then Window > Extensions > Pluck.


IF YOUR COMPUTER BLOCKS THE INSTALLER

Pluck isn't signed yet (that comes with version 1.0), so your computer may warn you.

  Mac: if it says the file can't be opened, open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
       scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to "Install Pluck.command".
  Windows: if a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears,
       click "More info", then "Run anyway".


UNINSTALL

Delete this folder, then restart Premiere Pro:
  Mac:      ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/com.dedkul.pluck
  Windows:  %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\com.dedkul.pluck


Problems or ideas: https://github.com/dedkul/pluck/issues
Only download videos you have the right to use.
